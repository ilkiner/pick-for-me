Pick For Me

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Effective: October 2026

Short version: You can use Pick For Me without an account; your lists and history then stay on your device. If you sign in, they are synced to our database in the EU. Ads (free version) are served by Google AdMob, and in the EU/UK we ask for your consent first. Anonymous usage statistics are optional and stay off unless you allow them. We do not sell your data.

1. Who is responsible

Pick For Me (“we”, “us”) is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: pickforme.app@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Without an account (guest): your saved lists, results history and settings are stored only on your device. Ads, purchases, crash reports and (if you allow them) usage statistics are processed as described below.
  • Account data (only if you sign in): email address and a securely hashed password, or, with “Continue with Google”, your Google account ID, name, email address and profile photo URL.
  • Synced content (only if you sign in): your saved lists and results history.
  • Purchases: subscription status and purchase history, managed by RevenueCat and Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: error reports, device model, OS and app version. IP addresses are not attached to reports.
  • Usage statistics (optional): which features are used (for example “result generated” or “paywall viewed”), linked to a random identifier and never to your name, email or account.
  • Advertising data (free version): advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interactions, collected by Google AdMob.

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are off by default, and we only ask for the notification permission after you turn them on.

3. Service providers and why we use them

  • Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC): showing and measuring ads in the free version. Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads and for storing information on your device (EU/UK, collected through Google’s consent form); legitimate interests for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention elsewhere. Privacy policy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc., USA): managing subscriptions and restoring purchases. Legal basis: performance of the contract (your subscription). Privacy policy
  • Sentry (Functional Software Inc.; EU data region, Germany): crash and error reporting. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure. Privacy policy
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc.; EU cloud): optional anonymous usage statistics. Location lookup from IP addresses is disabled. Legal basis: your consent where required (EU/UK); you can switch it off anytime in Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”. Privacy policy
  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.; hosted on AWS in Frankfurt, Germany): accounts, sign-in and syncing your lists and history. Row-level security means only you can read your data. Legal basis: performance of the contract (the account features you asked for). Privacy policy
  • Google Sign-In (Google): only if you choose “Continue with Google”. Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. Legal basis: performance of the contract. Privacy policy
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): the email (SMTP) provider Supabase uses to send account emails such as email verification and password reset. It receives your email address only. Legal basis: performance of the contract. Privacy policy

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data for third-party marketing. Pro subscribers see no ads, and no ad requests are made for them.

4. International transfers

Supabase, Sentry, PostHog and Brevo process data in the EU. Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States; these transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep data

  • On your device: results history up to 10 days (the free version shows the last 48 hours); saved lists until you delete them. Signing out removes lists and history from the device.
  • Account and synced data: until you delete it or delete your account. Cloud history is capped at the most recent 1,000 entries.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: up to 12 months, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Purchase records: as long as accounting and tax law requires.

6. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your synced lists and history, your account and your RevenueCat customer record.
  • Without the app: use the account deletion page or email pickforme.app@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. We respond within 30 days.
  • Guest data lives only on your device; uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent (EU/UK): change or withdraw it anytime in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Usage statistics: switch off in Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to pickforme.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored in the Android Keystore via SecureStore. Cloud data is protected by row-level security so each user can access only their own records.

9. Children

Pick For Me is suitable for everyone but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Ads are limited to content rated “G” (general audiences). If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The “Last updated” date above always shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or data requests: pickforme.app@gmail.com
